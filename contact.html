Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book?

For weddings, I recommend booking 6-12 months in advance. For smaller events, 2-3 months is usually sufficient.

Do you travel for events?

Yes! I'm happy to travel for destination weddings and special events. Travel fees apply for events outside the local area.

What's included in your packages?

Each package is customized to your needs, but typically includes vendor coordination, timeline management, and day-of coordination.
